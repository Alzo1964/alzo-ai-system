# Campaign Landing Pages

## Purpose

WordPress can host campaign landing pages for Campaign Studio when speed, editing convenience, and public presentation are the priorities.

## Landing Page Role

Campaign landing pages should convert defined audiences through focused messaging, clear offers, and measurable calls to action. They should be connected to ALZO campaign strategy rather than created as isolated pages.

## Page Requirements

Each landing page should define:

- Campaign objective.
- Target audience.
- Offer or conversion action.
- Message hierarchy.
- Required assets.
- Tracking requirements.
- Launch and review dates.

## Recommended Sections

- Hero statement and primary call to action.
- Problem, promise, and positioning.
- Proof, credibility, or examples.
- Offer details.
- FAQ or objection handling.
- Final call to action.

## Backend Connection

Landing pages should use backend APIs or plugin settings to receive campaign metadata, approved copy, tracking IDs, and conversion configuration. Form submissions and conversion events should return to the backend for reporting.

## Security and Compliance

- Validate form submissions.
- Protect personally identifiable information.
- Use trusted form handlers or secured custom endpoints.
- Avoid exposing private campaign strategy or internal notes.
- Maintain clear consent language where data is collected.

## Migration Flexibility

Landing page content and campaign metadata should remain portable. If campaigns later move to a headless frontend or dedicated funnel platform, WordPress pages should be exportable without losing strategy, copy, or performance context.
