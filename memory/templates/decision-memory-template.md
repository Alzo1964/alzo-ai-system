## Reason for Decision

Explain why this decision was made, not only what was decided.

Useful rationale may include:

- Strategic alignment.
- Operational simplicity.
- Risk reduction.
- Cost, time, or resource constraints.
- Long-term scalability.
- Repository structure or source-of-truth implications.
- Human, personal, or continuity considerations.
