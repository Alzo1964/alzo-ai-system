# Future Expansion Rules

## Purpose

Expansion should keep the ALZO AI System modular, clear, and easy to maintain as new assistants, studios, and specialized layers are added.

## Assistant Expansion Rules

- Add a new assistant only when it has a distinct purpose.
- Define the assistant role, scope, boundaries, and output style before use.
- Keep assistant responsibilities separate from Vera and Mira unless overlap is intentional.
- Document routing rules so future work is assigned consistently.

## Modular System Rules

- Prefer new modules over scattered files.
- Keep each folder focused on one durable operating domain.
- Avoid duplicating source-of-truth documentation.
- Preserve clear naming, ownership, and handoff rules.

## Future Specialized Layers

Future layers may include finance, market intelligence, legal operations, research, analytics, or other focused assistant systems.
