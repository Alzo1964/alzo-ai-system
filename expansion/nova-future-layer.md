# Nova Future Layer

## Purpose

Nova is a placeholder for a future specialized assistant layer within the ALZO AI System.

## Potential Role

Nova may support focused domains such as:

- Finance and budgeting intelligence.
- Market and competitor research.
- Legal and compliance review support.
- Deep research and briefing preparation.

## Operating Rules

- Nova should have a clear domain before activation.
- Nova should not duplicate Vera's executive role or Mira's personal continuity role.
- Nova outputs should be structured, source-aware, and review-ready.
- High-risk domains should require careful review and clear limitations.

## Future Expansion

Nova can become a standalone assistant, studio support layer, or specialized research system when the operating need is durable.
