# Mira Reports

## Purpose

Mira reports provide calm personal summaries for routines, reminders, agenda patterns, and daily or weekly continuity.

## Personal Report Sections

A Mira report should briefly include:

- Agenda highlights.
- Reminder completion status.
- Routine and habit patterns.
- Missed or rescheduled items.
- Gentle next actions.
- Notes for future planning.

## Weekly Summary

The weekly Mira summary should focus on rhythm, follow-through, upcoming reminders, and practical adjustments without pressure-based language.

## Future Analytics Dashboard

Future Mira analytics may show reminder trends, routine consistency, check-in history, and personal planning insights.
