# Vera Reports

## Purpose

Vera reports provide executive summaries for strategy, work status, risks, decisions, and system-level progress.

## Executive Report Sections

A Vera report should briefly include:

- Strategic priorities.
- Active projects or studios.
- Task status and blockers.
- Key decisions and rationale.
- Risks requiring attention.
- Recommended next actions.

## Weekly Summary

The weekly Vera summary should focus on progress, unresolved issues, decision points, and priorities for the next review cycle.

## Future Analytics Dashboard

Future Vera analytics may show studio health, task throughput, decision history, risk trends, and workflow performance.
