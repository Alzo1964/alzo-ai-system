# Reporting Architecture

## Purpose

The reporting layer turns ALZO AI System activity into concise summaries, review documents, and decision-ready operational insight.

## Report Types

- Vera executive reports.
- Mira personal reports.
- Weekly summaries.
- Task history reports.
- Activity and assistant interaction summaries.

## Weekly Summaries

Weekly summaries should highlight completed work, active priorities, unresolved blockers, upcoming reminders, and recommended next actions.

## Data Sources

Reports may draw from task history, activity logs, assistant interaction logs, reminders, decisions, and future database records.

## Future Analytics Dashboard

Future dashboards may provide filtered report views, trends, exports, and role-specific analytics for Vera and Mira.
